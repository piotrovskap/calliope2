# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Between: Revenue Lab LLC ("Service Provider")

And: Digital Airstrike ("Client")

Effective Date: \[Insert Start Date\]

## **1. Scope of Services**

Revenue Lab agrees to produce and deliver hyper-personalized videos designed to support automotive dealership sales and service campaigns. This includes but is not limited to:

- Personalized video content for individual leads based on CRM data

- Sales campaign videos (vehicle offers, equity positions, lease turn-ins)

- Service-related videos (reminders, missed appointments, declined services)

- Video formats optimized for email, text, web, and social distribution

## **2. Service Levels**

### **2.1 Turnaround Time**

<div class="table-wrap">

|                                      |                        |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------|
| Task                                 | SLA Timeframe          |
| Initial campaign setup (per dealer)  | Within 5 business days |
| Monthly campaign updates             | Within 3 business days |
| New video variants (template-based)  | Within 48 hours        |
| Custom video scripting or edits      | Within 5 business days |
| Emergency dealer requests (critical) | Within 24 hours        |

</div>

### **2.2 Availability**

Revenue Lab’s support team will be available Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM EST.\
Emergency support via dedicated contact for critical dealer issues outside standard hours.

## **3. Quality Standards**

- All videos will be rendered in HD format (1080p or better)

- Each video will meet Digital Airstrike's branding and compliance requirements

- All content is personalized based on dealership-provided CRM or DMS data (via secure upload or integration)

## **4. Performance Metrics**

- Video Delivery Accuracy: 98% of video outputs must reflect correct personalization tags

- Rendering Success Rate: 99% of generated videos must render without errors

- Client Satisfaction Score (CSAT): Target score ≥ 90% (based on Digital Airstrike internal reviews)

## **5. Data Handling & Confidentiality**

- Revenue Lab will maintain data confidentiality in accordance with SOC 2 standards

- No client data will be shared or stored beyond campaign usage

- Personalized data will be purged upon campaign completion or by written request

## **6. Issue Resolution**

<div class="table-wrap">

|  |  |  |  |
|----|----|----|----|
| Priority | Definition | Response Time | Resolution Time |
| Critical | Platform outage, major dealer disruption | 1 hour | 12 hours |
| High | Broken video personalization, data mismatch | 4 hours | 1 business day |
| Medium | Minor formatting, non-blocking issues | 1 business day | 3 business days |
| Low | Cosmetic edits, non-urgent tweaks | 1 business day | 5 business days |

</div>

## **7. Reporting**

Monthly performance reports will be delivered, detailing:

- Number of videos rendered per dealer

- Personalization success rates

- Engagement data (if available)

- Support tickets opened/resolved

## **8. Exclusions**

- Revenue Lab is not responsible for:

- Delays caused by incomplete or inaccurate dealership data

- Technical issues arising from third-party CRM/DMS systems

- Custom feature development outside agreed scope

## **9. Term & Termination**

This SLA remains in effect until superseded by a new agreement or terminated with 30 days’ written notice by either party.
