2.  Once you login to Reynolds and Reynolds, you’ll be dropped into this page. ​You’ll need to click on the hamburger menu to view into the next parts of the platform.​ After you click on the hamburger menu in the top right corner, click on Collections under Reports.

3.  Click on ‘Add’ to create a new report in Reynolds.

    1.  <span class="confluence-embedded-file-wrapper image-center-wrapper confluence-embedded-manual-size"><img src="https://digitalairstrike.atlassian.net/wiki/download/attachments/3475144705/image-20250911-202447.png?api=v2" class="confluence-embedded-image image-center" width="712" alt="image-20250911-202447.png" /></span>

4.  **Group**: This is your dealership filter. You’ll be able to select your primary data source based on this Group selection. Some dealerships will be grouped together by OEM. For instance, an auto group might have 3 Nissan dealers and they all might be listed under Antwerpen Nissan. ​

    1.  **Primary Category**: This is your customer segment. For the purpose of this process, we only pull Prospects from the CRM. ​

    2.  **Collection Name**: This is the name of your report. I normally name my reports: 3 Birds - Prospects – \[Dealer Name\] – \[CLIENTID\]

    3.  <span class="confluence-embedded-file-wrapper image-center-wrapper confluence-embedded-manual-size"><img src="https://digitalairstrike.atlassian.net/wiki/download/attachments/3475144705/image-20250911-202552.png?api=v2" class="confluence-embedded-image image-center" width="712" alt="image-20250911-202552.png" /></span>

5.  **Criteria:** *These are your primary conditions for your report. When you’re reading to add criteria, you will see ‘Add Criteria’. After you add your first criteria for your report, you’ll be able to select an option in the right-hand section.* You can use ‘Calculate Size’ in the bottom right to get an idea on the potential list size. If you need to set up additional filters or conditions on the data you want, you can set them up here. i.e., you want to exclude certain models or makes. ​

    1.  **Business Unit Name**: Dealership Name. ​

    2.  **Prospect Create Date**: I typically pull a historical, to track potential changes based on lead status. ​

    3.  **Desired Exterior Color**: Exclude containing ‘/’ to prevent data processing issues.​

    4.  <span class="confluence-embedded-file-wrapper image-center-wrapper confluence-embedded-manual-size"><img src="https://digitalairstrike.atlassian.net/wiki/download/attachments/3475144705/image-20250911-202625.png?api=v2" class="confluence-embedded-image image-center" width="712" alt="image-20250911-202625.png" /></span>

6.  **Your reports are now created. By default, reports are organized by the name alphabetically.**

    1.  Click on the report row, but not the actual name to run, and then click on ‘Export’. Clicking on the report name, you’ll be taken to modify the report filters, and criteria.

    2.  **You will need to click on a specific list of fields and in a very specific order. If you miss any of the fields or click them out of order, the processing will fail. You only need to select these fields once. Any time after, will save those list of fields. Next slide for fields.**​
